Youtube Lucky

Youtube Lucky is a private desktop tool used by one small team to manage and upload videos to the team's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-up.

What the tool does

  • Uploads a finished video file from the team's computer to the team's own channel, with title, description, tags, privacy or schedule settings, a thumbnail and an English subtitle file.
  • Reads the name of the connected channel so the operator can check the video goes to the right channel.
  • Every upload is started by a person after reviewing a confirmation screen. Nothing is posted automatically.

Google permissions used

  • youtube.upload: upload videos to the connected channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl: set the thumbnail, upload captions and read the channel name.
